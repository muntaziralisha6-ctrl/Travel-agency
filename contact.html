Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our services and travel planning.

How far in advance should I book my trip?

We recommend booking at least 3-6 months in advance for international trips and 1-3 months for domestic travel. This ensures the best availability for flights, accommodations, and activities, especially during peak seasons.

What is your cancellation policy?

Our cancellation policy varies depending on the package and suppliers. Generally, cancellations made 30+ days before departure receive a full refund minus a processing fee. Within 30 days, refunds depend on supplier policies. We always recommend purchasing travel insurance for maximum protection.

Do you offer travel insurance?

Yes, we partner with leading travel insurance providers to offer comprehensive coverage options. We can help you select the right plan for your needs, covering trip cancellation, medical emergencies, lost luggage, and more.

Can you help with visa applications?

Absolutely! We provide visa assistance services for most destinations. Our experts will guide you through the application process, help with documentation, and provide up-to-date information on visa requirements for your specific nationality and destination.

Do you create custom travel itineraries?

Yes, we specialize in creating personalized travel experiences. Our travel experts will work with you to design a custom itinerary that matches your interests, budget, and travel style. From luxury getaways to adventure trips, we'll craft the perfect journey just for you.
